# Ergonomics of Gorilla Arm & Mid-Air Micro-Gestures

## Summary
Investigating the physical limits of direct spatial interaction. Direct touch in mid-air quickly fatigues the user's upper arms. 

## Key Recommendation
1. Keep frequent actions within the comfortable **neutral zone** (forearms horizontal or rested).
2. Pair eye gaze for target selection with subtle finger pinches for activation.
