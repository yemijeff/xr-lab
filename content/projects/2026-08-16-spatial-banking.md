# Spatial Portfolio: 3D Financial Telemetry Workspace

## Context & Core Question
*Why does this need to be in XR?*
Financial traders and risk managers monitor dozens of interconnected asset classes simultaneously. Flat 2D screens require endless tab switching and high cognitive context overhead.

In spatial computing, depth and volume allow simultaneous representation of correlation, volatility, and order flow without screen clutter.
