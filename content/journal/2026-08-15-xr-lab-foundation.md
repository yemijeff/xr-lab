# XR Lab Foundation: Setting up the Spatial Design Architecture

Today marks the initial foundation of **XR Lab**. As a Product Designer stepping into the spatial realm, documenting the cognitive and interaction paradigm shifts is as crucial as building the prototypes themselves.

## Why this structure?
- **Separation of Workspace and Showcase**: Having a dedicated private dashboard prevents half-baked experiments from littering the public portfolio, while keeping the publishing friction close to zero.
- **Git-Backed Portability**: All long-form reflections and experiments are versioned directly in Markdown with structured frontmatter.
